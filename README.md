# 🤖 Sri Sai AI Tuitions

**Sri Sai AI Tuitions** is an online learning initiative designed to help students learn **Artificial Intelligence, modern technology tools, and digital skills** in a simple and practical way.

This platform focuses on guiding students to understand **AI tools, automation technologies, and real-world tech applications** that are useful for education, career development, and future opportunities.

🌐 Live Website
https://srinathavula.github.io/sri-sai-ai-tuitions/

---

## 🎯 Mission

Our mission is to make **Artificial Intelligence learning accessible and practical** for students by teaching modern tools and technologies in a beginner-friendly way.

We aim to help students:

* Understand AI tools and technologies
* Learn practical digital skills
* Improve productivity using AI
* Explore modern tech careers

## 🚀 Vision

Sri Sai AI Tuitions is an online learning platform designed to help students explore modern technology through the power of Artificial Intelligence. Our mission is to make learning smarter, faster, and more accessible using AI tools, simple explanations, and practical examples.

We focus on teaching AI tools for productivity, basic coding concepts, and the latest technology trends to help learners enhance their knowledge and develop future-ready skills. 💻✨

Sri Sai AI Tuitions aims to build a strong learning community where students can learn AI, technology, and automation skills to prepare for the future digital world. 🌍📈
